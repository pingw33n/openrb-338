## Final Summary

<!-- SECTION:FINAL_SUMMARY:BEGIN -->
The PCF (crates/rb-engine/src/pcf.rs, CPCFInsert 0x100166D0) and the compressor (crates/rb-engine/src/comp.rs, CCompInsert mono 0x1000EC70, stereo 0x1000EDE0) are implemented from the disassembly and in Devices::implemented. The CLI's "not implemented" warning is gone.

Result: all 22 diag songs that route the PCF or the compressor are byte-exact against tests/refs at --idle-blocks 9088 (before: all 22 differed with the bypass placeholders), and so are all 8 shipped songs (sha256 and frames). One hypothesis failed along the way: the release constant was first mis-decoded (0x3F7FE724 instead of 0x3F7F9724); it is fixed.

The PCF's divide by zero: the wave byte indexes the pattern data unchecked, and 17 waves (55, 111 and 113..127) read a pattern length of 0. The step's idiv (0x10016811) then faults in every block, and SafeSoundCallback's catch(...) (0x1001B055) ends each call before the int16 conversion, so the buffer is never written. This is why the wave-127 songs are silent; task-039 is settled in its notes but not marked Done.
- The engine models it as frame::Fault; Engine::fault() records it, and the rb CLI prints a warning but still exits 0 and writes the original's output.
- An export renders all its calls into one buffer, as rbhost (chunk_buf) and the app (0x427C2F, 0x4281C3) do, so a faulting chunk repeats the previous one.
- The raw bytes 0x10033310..0x10034400 are a generated static table, PCF_DATA. By the user's decision of 2026-10-27, it stays embedded with its dependency on the preferred base documented: 171 relocated pointer words, and 57 waves with one among their step bytes.

The mixer's change bytes are panel-only: their only reader is slot 13 sub_10015D40, and the panel's own set message is msg 145. They are not modelled.

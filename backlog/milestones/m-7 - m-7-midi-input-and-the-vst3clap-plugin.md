## Description

MIDI remote control and live play of the devices, MIDI clock sync in the standalone app, and orb as a VST3/CLAP instrument plugin (nih-plug). Behavior follows the original where reverse engineering shows it, but MIDI-driven output does not have to match the original sample for sample.
